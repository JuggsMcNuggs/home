// jess

My name is Jess. I'm 4'4". I'm a little person. I have a bone condition that even I can't pronounce. Congenital hypophosphatemic rickets. Basically, my body can't hold onto the minerals it needs to build bones properly. My bones are just bad. That's the short version. The long version involves words I can't pronounce, 2 surgeries before kindergarten, and a body that processes vitamins like it's never heard of them. I'm bow legged with old lady bones and the structural integrity of a wet paper towel. But I'm charming so it evens out. I had my daughter at 17 and she was taller than me by the time she was 8. That's just life over here.

Whether it's creating content here and there (yes, even there..), creating RP moments in GTA between all my roles, or getting way too invested in a community I've been part of for years. I can't say no to most things when it comes to GTA RP. I'm too addicted. I've been playing in Highlife for years and ended up as a Casino Director, a VU Supervisor, a moderator, and on the Events Team. All at the same time. Don't ask me how that happened.

My brain never shuts off, and I don't care if it's 4 AM and I have an idea. Sleep can wait. That's how I ended up with an AI who runs my life. His name is Levi.
